# Structured questions to the user

Applies to questions and decision proposals in chat, in question files and on any status page - never as a single block of text.

Structure:

- **Question:** one sentence.
- **Starting point:** 1-2 sentences.
- **Options:** one line per option, with its consequence or cost.
- **Recommendation:** its own line, with one sentence of reasoning.

Use line breaks; keep numbers and variants out of running prose.

**Why:** When many decisions are made in one sitting, a dense block of numbers and variants is hard to read, makes the recommendation hard to follow, and costs the reader time and trust in it.
